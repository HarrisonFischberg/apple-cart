Build battery care. A privileged helper installed only when the first battery tool is enabled; it accepts commands only from the signed app, is verified after every macOS update with a one-click repair, and is removed with the battery reset to 100%. Tools: charge limit, heat protection, sailing range, discharge to a level, top up once, stop charging in sleep, keep limit after quit, calibration cycle, schedule with catch-up, MagSafe light. The Battery tab always states the current charging state and the specific reason charging is paused. Read-only tools without the helper: battery details with 24h history, power flow in watts, true percentage, energy-hungry apps. Detect unsupported Macs or OS versions that block charge control and show a clear message instead of failing silently.
